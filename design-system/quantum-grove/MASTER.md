# Quantum Grove design system

User direction: quantum computing and computer science, with light traveling through a network. The product name is Quantum Grove; botanical styling is intentionally replaced by a circuit and state-space identity.

## Visual system

- Canvas: `#0B1120`; sidebar: `#0E1729`; surfaces: `#121C30`.
- Foreground: `#E9EFFF`; secondary text: `#A1B0CC`; border: `#283751`.
- Signal cyan: `#63D8F5`; interference violet: `#A79BFF`; warning amber: `#FFD18B`.
- Headings: Outfit, medium and semibold. Body: DM Sans, regular and medium. Monospace is reserved for mathematical state labels and circuit data.
- The hero's circuit visualization is the signature element. Cyan and violet pulses show conceptual flow through gates; it is an illustration, not a hardware claim.
- Keep most surfaces flat. Reserve glow for a small signal, the current learning state, and the hero illustration.

## Interaction and hierarchy

The next lesson is the single main action. Goals and course milestones provide context. Course details, prerequisites, outcomes, and catalog filters use progressive disclosure. Keep quiz answers, account controls, and simulations fully functional.

Use visible focus states and semantic buttons. Light text on dark surfaces must meet 4.5:1 for body copy. Respect reduced-motion preferences; no flashing. Mobile layouts must fit 375px without horizontal scrolling. Touch controls should be at least 44px. Do not copy a franchise's logos, screens, or other distinctive assets.

## UI UX Pro findings

The quantum-computing query recommended a dark interface with cyan and interference purple. The implementation moderates those colors and retains proportional body typography to support long educational content. The earlier generic educational recommendation (comic type and a green light palette) does not apply to the user's revised brief.
